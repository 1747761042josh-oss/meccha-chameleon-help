## Quick Answer

Players have reported that MECCHA CHAMELEON can stay on `signing in...` and never enter the game. This site has not reproduced the issue, so the best starting point is to check your Steam session, restart the client, and see whether other players are reporting server problems at the same time.

## Symptoms

- The game stays on `signing in...`.
- The main menu or matchmaking does not load.
- The player cannot do anything after opening the game.

## Reported Player Question

One Steam report says: "I opened the game, but the screen is stuck on signing in and I can't play. I can't do anything."

## Safe First Checks

1. Restart the game.
2. Restart Steam.
3. Check whether the Steam client is online.
4. Check whether other online Steam games can connect.
5. Disable VPN or proxy temporarily if you use one.
6. Wait and retry later if many players are reporting server problems.

## If It Still Does Not Work

Record the exact time, region, Steam account state, and whether the game ever reaches the main menu. If the issue appears for many players at once, it may be server-side rather than something you can fix locally.

## What Not to Do

- Do not delete random system files.
- Do not install unofficial launchers.
- Do not change account credentials based on comments from unknown users.
- Do not use authentication bypass tools.
