## Quick Answer

This MVP has not personally tested friend invites. Keep the first checks simple: confirm both players are online in Steam, both are on the same game version, and neither player is using modified files.

## Symptoms

- A friend invite does not open the lobby.
- Joining fails without a clear error.
- A lobby appears visible to one player but not another.

## Safe First Checks

1. Make sure both players restart Steam.
2. Confirm both players have updated the game.
3. Try creating a new lobby instead of reusing an old one.
4. Check whether public matchmaking works.
5. Record whether the issue happens with every friend or only one account.

## What Not to Do

- Do not disable security software permanently.
- Do not open router ports unless an official source says it is required.
- Do not use third-party matchmaking tools.
