## Quick Answer

Several players report Workshop maps not loading, not being detected, or not downloading correctly in MECCHA CHAMELEON. This site has not reproduced the issue, so start with safe Steam Workshop checks before changing files manually.

## Symptoms

- Workshop maps do not appear when creating a server.
- A custom map download button does not open or does not work.
- Subscribed maps are not detected by the game.
- The game may say the player does not own or has not downloaded a map.

## Reported Player Questions

- "The workshop maps aren't loading for me. I deleted and reinstalled them, but they're still not detected."
- "Workshop map don't show up when I create a server."
- "I am subscribed to many workshop maps and have played them all but sometimes when starting the game it says I do not own them."

## Safe First Checks

1. Restart Steam.
2. Open the MECCHA CHAMELEON Workshop page in Steam and confirm you are subscribed to the map.
3. Wait for Steam to finish downloading Workshop content.
4. Launch MECCHA CHAMELEON from Steam after the download is complete.
5. Try one known official map to confirm the base game still loads correctly.
6. If the problem only happens in a friend's lobby, ask whether the host changed maps recently.

## If It Still Does Not Work

Record whether the map appears in Steam's subscribed items, whether Steam downloads anything after subscribing, and whether the problem affects all Workshop maps or only one map.

## What Not to Do

- Do not install Workshop maps from random websites.
- Do not manually delete Steam Workshop folders unless you know how Steam will restore them.
- Do not assume every custom-map issue is a game bug; Steam Workshop rate limits and download state can also be involved.
