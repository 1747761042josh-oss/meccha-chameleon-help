## Quick Answer

Players report that a black screen can happen after joining a server, especially around custom or modded maps. A community reply suggests the map may not be downloaded or the client may not be updated, but this site has not tested that explanation.

## Symptoms

- The lobby appears colorful or normal.
- When the match starts, the map is black or does not render correctly.
- One report says the player can see everyone without proper walls or rendering.
- The issue may happen after joining a server using a custom map.

## Safe First Checks

1. Leave the server and join a standard map lobby.
2. If it is a custom map, confirm that the Workshop map is actually downloaded.
3. Restart Steam so Workshop downloads can refresh.
4. Check whether MECCHA CHAMELEON has a pending update.
5. Avoid staying in a match where the map did not load correctly, because the round may be unfair for other players.

## If It Still Does Not Work

Record whether the issue happens only on Workshop maps or also on official maps. That distinction matters: Workshop download failures and normal graphics/rendering problems need different fixes.

## What Not to Do

- Do not exploit a black-screen or no-wall state to win matches.
- Do not manually delete Workshop folders unless you know exactly what Steam will re-download.
- Do not install unofficial map files from random websites.
