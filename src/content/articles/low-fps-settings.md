## Quick Answer

Some players report extremely low FPS, including 4-5 FPS even when changing settings. Evidence is still thin, so this page is marked Unconfirmed and only lists safe performance checks.

## Symptoms

- Very low FPS during gameplay.
- Changing from low to high settings does not seem to change performance.
- Players ask for more graphics options for weaker PCs.
- Some players mention discomfort from camera or field-of-view limitations.

## Safe First Checks

1. Restart the game after changing graphics settings.
2. Confirm the game is using the dedicated GPU if your laptop has one.
3. Close screen recorders, overlays, browser tabs, and background launchers.
4. Update GPU drivers from the official NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel website.
5. Lower resolution before changing more advanced options.
6. Test in an official map before testing Workshop maps.

## If It Still Does Not Work

Record your CPU, GPU, RAM, Windows version, resolution, map type, and whether FPS is low in menus or only during matches. Without that information, it is hard to separate hardware limits from game-side optimization issues.

## What Not to Do

- Do not download FPS booster executables.
- Do not install unofficial driver packs.
- Do not edit game configuration files unless you can reverse the change.
- Do not follow registry tweaks from unrelated games.
