## Quick Answer

Players are reporting voice chat problems, including voice chat not working and people sounding robotic. This site has not tested the microphone system, so use normal Windows and Steam audio checks first.

## Symptoms

- Voice chat does not work in a lobby.
- Other players cannot hear you.
- Player voices sound robotic or distorted.
- Players ask for microphone sensitivity options.

## Safe First Checks

1. Check that Windows is using the correct input device.
2. Check Steam voice settings and confirm your microphone works there.
3. Restart MECCHA CHAMELEON after changing audio devices.
4. Make sure your headset is not muted physically.
5. Try another lobby to separate a lobby-specific issue from a local microphone issue.

## If It Still Does Not Work

Record whether your microphone works in Steam voice chat, Discord, or another game. If the microphone works elsewhere but fails only in MECCHA CHAMELEON, the issue is more likely game-specific.

## What Not to Do

- Do not install random audio drivers from unofficial sites.
- Do not give microphone permissions to unknown apps.
- Do not permanently disable Windows audio protections to test one game.
