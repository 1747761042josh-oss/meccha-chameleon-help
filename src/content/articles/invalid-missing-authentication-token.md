## Quick Answer

Several Steam players report an error saying MECCHA CHAMELEON failed because of an invalid or missing authentication token. This site has not personally reproduced the issue, so treat the steps below as safe first checks, not a guaranteed fix.

Start by launching the game from the Steam library, restarting Steam, checking that you are logged into the correct Steam account, and avoiding any unofficial authentication workaround.

## Symptoms

- The game shows `Failed due to invalid or missing authentication token for user`.
- The error may appear as soon as the player enters or launches the game.
- Some players describe it as a sign-in or login validation failure.

## Reported Player Questions

- "Why does this appear as soon as I enter the game?"
- "I am having this issue when I launch the game."
- "Game failing due to invalid or missing authentication token, can't sign in."
- "How do I fix this?"

## Safe First Checks

1. Fully close MECCHA CHAMELEON.
2. Quit Steam from the system tray, then open Steam again.
3. Confirm the correct Steam account is logged in.
4. Launch MECCHA CHAMELEON from the Steam library instead of a shortcut.
5. Check whether Steam online services are unstable.
6. Verify the game files from Steam if the issue started after an update.

## If It Still Does Not Work

Collect the exact error text, launch time, region, and whether Steam friends/matchmaking are working in other games. The error may require a game-side or Steam-side fix if many players are seeing it at the same time.

## What Not to Do

- Do not download token repair tools.
- Do not use cracked or modified game files.
- Do not paste Steam credentials into third-party pages.
- Do not follow instructions that claim to bypass authentication.
- Do not edit account, token, or Steam files based on random comments.
