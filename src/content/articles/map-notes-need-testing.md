## Quick Answer

The maps section should not publish best hiding spots until there are original screenshots, repeatable test notes, and a game version record.

## Required Before Publishing Map Tips

1. Screenshot of the location.
2. Map name.
3. Game version.
4. Hider color or paint setup.
5. Number of test rounds.
6. Whether seekers found the spot.
7. Notes on why the spot worked or failed.

## Why This Matters

Map strategies become outdated quickly after patches or after players learn common hiding routes. Without testing, a map article can mislead readers faster than a normal news post.
